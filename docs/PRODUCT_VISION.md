# RecruiterReply.com Product Vision

## Mission

RecruiterReply.com helps job seekers make better career decisions and helps recruiters engage candidates more effectively using AI-powered analysis, negotiation assistance, and career intelligence.

The goal is to create a SaaS platform that generates recurring subscription revenue while solving real problems experienced by recruiters and job seekers.

---

## Target Audiences

### Job Seekers

Professionals seeking:

* Better recruiter communications
* Salary negotiation assistance
* Offer comparison tools
* Career decision support
* Recruiter relationship management

### Recruiters

Agency and corporate recruiters seeking:

* Better candidate engagement
* Resume-to-job matching
* Candidate qualification assistance
* Compensation guidance
* Recruiting analytics

---

## Phase 1 MVP

### AI Recruiter Message Analyzer

User pastes recruiter email.

System provides:

* Market competitiveness analysis
* Compensation evaluation
* Red flag detection
* Suggested response

### AI Reply Generator

Generate:

* Interested responses
* Decline responses
* Counteroffer responses
* Follow-up responses

### Offer Comparison Tool

Compare:

* Salary
* Hourly rate
* Benefits
* Commute
* Remote flexibility
* Contract length

### Candidate CRM

Track:

* Recruiters
* Companies
* Opportunities
* Interviews
* Follow-ups
